# Infrastructure Projects — City of Dasmariñas

This page tracks **ongoing and recently completed infrastructure projects** funded by the City Government of Dasmariñas, the national government, and other sources.

Under the **Full Disclosure Policy**, bid results for infrastructure projects and contract awards must be publicly posted.

---

## 2026 Highlight Projects

### 🛣️ CALAX Subsection 3 — Silang–Dasmariñas (7.9 km)

**Status:** Finishing Stages | **Agency:** DPWH / MPCALA

Connects the Silang (Aguinaldo) Interchange to the Governor's Drive Interchange. As of 2026, lane markings and road signage are nearing completion. This section completes a key expressway link between Cavite's interior municipalities and Metro Manila via CAVITEX.

**Source:** [Manila Bulletin — DPWH: CALAX Cavite Segment on Track](https://mb.com.ph/2025/3/26/dpwh-calax-cavite-segment-on-track-for-completion-by-2025) · [PNA — PBBM Inspects Governor's Drive Interchange](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1246525)

### 🚆 LRT Line 6 — Niyog to Dasmariñas (19 km)

**Status:** ROW Acquisition | **Agency:** DOTr / LRTA

Extends LRT-1 from Bacoor (Niyog Station) southward to Dasmariñas City. The Governor's Drive Station is a key logistics hub for 2026. Right-of-way (ROW) acquisition is the current focus, with full operations targeted by 2028–2029.

**Source:** [Philstar — LRT-Cavite ROW Turnover Seen This Year (Feb 2026)](https://www.philstar.com/nation/2026/02/26/2510526/turnover-lrt-cavite-right-way-seen-year) · [Inquirer — 3 Big-Ticket Railway Projects Status](https://business.inquirer.net/433284/3-big-ticket-railway-projects-in-limbo-but-will-push-through-says-dotr)

### 🎓 UP-Dasmariñas Tech Innovation Campus — 6-Story R&D Facility

**Status:** Under Construction | **Agency:** UP / DPWH

A specialized 6-story research and development facility within the University of the Philippines Dasmariñas campus. Expected to complete structural work by late 2026, it will house technology innovation labs and collaborative research spaces for CALABARZON.

**Source:** [Philstar — UP Breaks Ground for Cavite Campus (Sep 2024)](https://www.philstar.com/nation/2024/09/19/2386254/breaks-ground-cavite-campus) · [UP System — UP-DPWH Infrastructure Partnership](https://up.edu.ph/up-dpwh-cement-partnership-to-implement-infra-projects/)

### 🏟️ Dasmariñas Arena & Sports Complex — 4,500–5,000 Seat Capacity

**Status:** Fully Operational | **Agency:** City Government of Dasmariñas

A world-class indoor arena hosting 16 sports disciplines. Now fully operational and serving as a venue for major collegiate leagues including NCRAA. The complex supports Dasmariñas' goal of becoming a regional sports hub in CALABARZON.

**Source:** [SPIN.ph — First PBA Games at Dasmariñas Arena (May 2024)](https://www.spin.ph/basketball/pba/pba-on-pace-for-good-sized-crowd-in-first-ever-game-in-dasmari-as-a795-20240522) · [PNA — City Arena of Dasmariñas Photo Feature](https://www.pna.gov.ph/photos/47948)

## Project Categories

The City Government funds infrastructure through:

| Funding Source                   | Project Types                                              |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **20% Development Fund (IRA)**   | Barangay roads, drainage, multi-purpose halls              |
| **DRRMF (5% of IRA)**            | Flood control, evacuation centers, early warning systems   |
| **General Fund**                 | City hall facilities, public markets, parks                |
| **DPWH (National)**              | National and provincial roads passing through the city     |
| **SEF (Special Education Fund)** | School buildings, classrooms, school facilities            |
| **LGU Loans / Bonds**            | Major capital projects (subject to Sangguniang approval)   |
| **PCSO / NHA / DSWD**            | Socialized housing, health facilities, social welfare hubs |

## Procurement Process

All infrastructure projects above the **Small Value Procurement** threshold undergo public bidding through the **Bids and Awards Committee (BAC)**:

1. **Project identification** — BAC prepares Annual Procurement Plan (APP)
2. **Advertisement** — Posted on PhilGEPS, city bulletin boards, and official website
3. **Pre-bid conference** — Open to all prospective bidders
4. **Bid submission and opening** — Public opening; results posted on FDP Portal
5. **Contract award** — Winning bidder notified; contract posted publicly
6. **Implementation** — With regular inspection by City Engineering Office
7. **Completion and turnover** — Documented and reported in Annual Accomplishment Report
